# Scope Statement
*(for Frontiers Research Topic 84224 — Perioperative Management and Clinical Challenges in Elderly Major Surgical Patients. Volume II)*

Our manuscript, *"Preoperative GNRI and NLR for Predicting the Need for Postoperative ICU-Level Monitoring in Older Orthopedic Surgical Patients: Development and Independent Validation,"* fits squarely within the scope of this Research Topic and its hosted sections (Frontiers in Medicine — Geriatric Medicine; Precision Medicine).

The work directly addresses two priority themes explicitly solicited by the Topic: **(1) perioperative risk prediction** and **(2) frailty and risk stratification in older surgical patients**. We developed and independently validated a parsimonious preoperative risk model that combines the Geriatric Nutritional Risk Index (GNRI) and the Neutrophil-to-Lymphocyte Ratio (NLR) to identify older adults at high risk of requiring postoperative ICU-level monitoring after major orthopedic surgery. This aligns with the Topic's emphasis on preoperative optimization, risk stratification, and early identification of high-risk geriatric populations to guide anesthetic planning and postoperative monitoring strategies.

Orthopedic surgery is expressly welcomed by the Topic as a major surgical specialty, and our multidisciplinary design — spanning geriatrics, orthopedics, and intensive care — embodies the cross-specialty collaboration the collection seeks to foster. By linking a modifiable nutritional–inflammatory phenotype to a hard postoperative outcome, the study provides an evidence-based, clinically actionable tool that supports enhanced recovery and improves perioperative outcomes in the aging surgical population, which is precisely the translational goal of this Research Topic.

**Keywords (aligned with Topic keywords):** Geriatric medicine; Perioperative care; Elderly surgical patients; Perioperative risk prediction; Risk stratification; Orthopedic surgery.
